PRIVACY

Your Homebridge data stays between you and your server.

Last updated: September 16, 2026

Product website

This product website is informational. It does not connect to your Homebridge, read your server configuration or request your Homebridge credentials.

Homebridge Manager

The development app connects directly to the Homebridge address entered by the user. Sign-in is performed against Homebridge Config UI X. The current prototype does not store your Homebridge password; the access token is intended for the current application session.

Local network and VPN

Homebridge Manager is designed for use on a trusted local network or through a private connection such as Tailscale. We do not recommend exposing the Homebridge administration port directly to the public internet.

Diagnostics and support

If you later share screenshots, logs or configuration when reporting a problem, remove passwords, tokens, internal addresses and other sensitive information before sending them. A public support channel will be announced before product release.

Analytics and cookies

The source version of this website contains no advertising or tracking scripts and does not use analytics cookies. The hosting platform or a future domain provider may process standard technical information under their own terms. This section will be updated before public commercial release to reflect the actual deployment.

Contact

An official contact for support and privacy questions will be published before commercial release. Do not send passwords, tokens or sensitive configurations to unverified contacts.
